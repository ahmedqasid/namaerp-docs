# Technician Appointments

If your business sells something and then has to turn up at the customer's address to make it work — install the split unit, assemble the wardrobe, take the sample — this is the part of the menu that plans the visit. You describe the work, group your field staff into crews, tell the system when those crews are available, and then book slots on a weekly calendar that shows in colour who is already promised elsewhere.

It is a self-contained booking system with its own licence, `crm-technician-appointments`, and no connection to the machine- or service-maintenance suites next door in this menu. Two things are worth knowing before you start: **an appointment books one crew, never two**, and **a technician belongs to exactly one crew** — most of the rules on these pages follow from those.
