# Project Management

Project Management (ECPA) is the Nama module for firms whose product is professional time rather than
goods: architecture and engineering offices, consultancies, audit and legal practices, design studios.
Its whole design follows a job from the offer that won it, through the phases it is delivered in, down
to the individual hours people spend on it — and back up again into an invoice the customer can be
shown.

If you are new to it, read the overview first. It explains what the module is for, why it is a
different thing from Contracting, and the vocabulary — types, disciplines, milestones and the matrix
they form — that every other page assumes you already have.

Required licence for everything below: **`ecpa`**. One code, all or nothing.
